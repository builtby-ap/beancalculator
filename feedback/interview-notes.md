# User Interview — မြန်မာပဲတွက်စနစ် (Myanmar Bean Calculator)

- **Who:** My aunt (family member — runs a small trading business)
- **When:** 2026-07-24
- **How:** In person — ~15 min

## What they do today (without your project)

They manually write vouchers/receipts by hand for every bean sale. It takes time, and there's always a risk of calculation errors when converting weights and deductions.

## What they liked

- Generating receipts/vouchers is **really comfortable** — saves a lot of time compared to manual writing
- The app is **effective for daily business** use
- Being able to auto-calculate the settlement (viss conversion, deductions) removes manual math errors

## What confused them / what's missing

- **Dashboard total amount is confusing** — it shows the total for all farmers combined, making it hard to see individual farmer balances
- **Payment confirmation unclear** — it's not obvious how to tell if a payment has been completed or is still pending

## What would make them actually use it

- Add per-farmer filtering on the dashboard so totals show per farmer, not just aggregate
- Add a clear payment status indicator (paid/unpaid) on transactions

## What I'll change (next steps)

- [ ] Add farmer filter on dashboard to show per-farmer totals
- [ ] Add payment status (paid/pending) to transactions
- [ ] Make payment confirmation more visible
